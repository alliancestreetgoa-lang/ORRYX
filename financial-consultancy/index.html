01 — What it covers

Four parts of the work.

Financial planning, budgets and forecasts

A budget and forecast the owners can actually run the business by, built on the plan and not on last year plus a percentage.

Cash-flow and working-capital reviews

Where cash comes in, where it gets stuck and how much the business needs to keep operating comfortably.

Feasibility studies for new ventures

Whether a new venture, product or location stacks up before money is committed to it.

Funding and investor readiness

Getting the numbers and the supporting information in order before the business approaches a lender or an investor.

02 — When it fits

Signs it is time to talk.

  • Profit looks fine but cash is always tight.
  • There is no budget the team actually works to.
  • A new venture needs testing before money goes in.
  • A lender or investor will soon ask hard questions.

05 — Questions

Financial consultancy, answered plainly.

What is financial consultancy?

Financial consultancy is advice on the numbers behind a business plan. At Orryx it covers financial planning, budgets and forecasts, cash-flow and working-capital reviews, feasibility studies for new ventures, and funding and investor readiness.

Is Orryx licensed for financial consultancy?

Financial consultancy is activity 6619.14, held by Orryx Commodities L.L.C-FZ under an Activity Authorisation issued on 18 November 2026 under Meydan Free Zone regulations, with expiry on 16 October 2027. The company's business licence number is 2538901.01.

How does financial consultancy connect to the other Orryx services?

Each activity feeds the next. The financial review shows what a strategy will cost and whether the business can afford it, and it supports the preparation of a business for sale when the owner decides to exit.
