03 — Method

How an engagement flows.

  1. 1

    Listen

    We start with the owner's real question — growth, control, cash or exit — and the facts behind it.

    Brief · data · interviews
  2. 2

    Diagnose

    We examine how the business is run and how the numbers behave, and separate symptoms from causes.

    Management + financial review
  3. 3

    Design

    We set out the options, a recommended strategy and the plan to reach it — in plain language.

    Strategy · roadmap · budget
  4. 4

    Deliver

    We stay alongside management while the plan is carried out, and adjust as results arrive.

    Implementation support
  5. 5

    Transact

    When buying or selling a business is the right move, our brokerage desk arranges the deal.

    Buyer–seller introduction
